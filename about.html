Our Story

Founded in 2025, Jnm Byte emerged from a shared vision to bridge the gap between cutting-edge academic research and real-world cybersecurity,Machine Learning, Software Development challenges.

Our team of computer science graduates from Laikipia University recognized the growing need for intelligent security solutions that could adapt to evolving threats in real-time.

Today, we combine deep expertise in machine learning, Software Development, Computer Networking and cybersecurity to deliver solutions that not only protect but also empower businesses to thrive in the digital age.
